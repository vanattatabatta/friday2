Copyright Protection

How we respect creators, and how to tell us when something in the library should not be there.

1Our approach

Students share notes, solutions and study guides here, and we want that to stay fair to the people who wrote the original material. Uploaders confirm they have the right to share what they add, and every upload goes through automatic checks before it appears. When a rights holder tells us something is wrong, we act on it.

2Reporting a copyright problem

If you own a work, or act for the owner, and you believe a document on Expert Help copies it without permission, send us a notice through the contact page with the subject line Copyright notice. Please include:

  • The address (URL) of each page you are reporting.
  • A description of the original work, and a link to it if it is online.
  • Your name, your role, and a way to reach you by email.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not allowed by the owner, the owner's agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  • Your typed or handwritten signature.

3What happens next

  • We review the notice promptly, usually within a few working days.
  • If it is complete and the claim looks valid, we take the document down and tell the member who uploaded it.
  • We may share your notice, without your private contact details where possible, with that member so they can respond.

4If your document was removed

If you believe a removal was a mistake, you can send us a counter-notice through the contact page. It should name the removed document, explain why you are allowed to share it, include your contact details and confirm that you accept responsibility for the statement. If the rights holder does not take legal action in a reasonable time, we may restore the document.

5Repeat uploads

Members who upload infringing material more than once lose uploading rights and may have their account closed. We keep a record of who uploaded what, and from where, so we can deal with repeat cases fairly.

6Please be honest

Sending a notice you know is false can have legal consequences. If you are unsure whether something is infringing, ask us before filing.
