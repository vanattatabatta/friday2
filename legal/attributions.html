1Why this page exists

We build on the work of many open-source projects and service providers. We are grateful to them, and this page lists the main ones.

2Services we connect to

  • Google Sign-In, for the Continue with Google button.
  • Stripe, for secure card checkout. Card numbers go to Stripe and never touch our servers.
  • Email delivery providers, for confirmation and service messages.
  • AI language-model providers, which power Ask AI, the AI Detector, the paraphraser and the other writing tools.

3Software and files

  • Font Awesome Free icons, used under their open licences.
  • Google Fonts typefaces, used under the SIL Open Font Licence.
  • LibreOffice and Poppler, which turn Word, Excel and PDF files into page previews.
  • Sharp, for resizing and blurring page images.
  • Node.js, Express and PostgreSQL, which run the site.
