Community Guidelines

A shared library works when people treat it, and each other, with respect.

1Be useful

  • Share notes and solutions that other students can really learn from.
  • Give files a clear title so people can find them.
  • Rate documents honestly with the thumbs buttons. A rating should reflect how helpful the file was.

2Be original

Upload your own work. Copies of material you do not own, files stitched together from other uploads and near-duplicates of documents already in the library will be refused.

3Be respectful

  • No harassment, hate, threats or targeting of anyone.
  • No sexual content, graphic violence or anything involving minors in unsafe ways.
  • No personal details about other people, including names with grades, ID numbers, addresses or medical records.

4Keep it clean

  • No spam, adverts, link dropping or search-engine tricks in files, titles or messages.
  • No malware, hidden scripts or files that try to harm a reader's device.
  • No fake accounts, vote rigging or unlock farming.

5What happens when rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may refuse a file, remove it, remove unlocks earned from it, limit uploading, suspend the account or close it. We do not always warn first when the harm is serious.

6Tell us about a problem

See something that does not belong? Use the contact page or the chat box and give us the document address. Reports are read by a person, and we do not tell the uploader who reported.

Questions about this page?Write to us and a person will reply by email.
