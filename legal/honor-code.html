1The pledge

When I use Expert Help I will:

  • Share only work that is mine, or that I have the right to share.
  • Learn from what I read, rather than copy it and pass it off as mine.
  • Treat other members' work and time with respect.
  • Follow my school's rules about outside help.
  • Report material that should not be here instead of ignoring it.

2Why we ask for it

A library that students trust is one where people give as well as take. The honor code is a reminder of that. It is not a legal contract on its own, but breaking it can lead to the steps described in our Community Guidelines.

3How it shows up on the site

  • Uploading asks you to confirm you have the right to share each file.
  • Unlocks are earned by contributing real, original documents.
  • Automatic checks and reports help keep the library honest for everyone.
