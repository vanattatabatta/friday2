Academic Integrity

Expert Help is for learning. Here is how we think about honest use of study material.

1Why this matters

Schools set their own rules about what help is allowed. We cannot know those rules for every course, so we ask each member to know theirs and follow them.

2How the library is meant to be used

  • Use documents to understand a topic, check your method or see how a good answer is structured.
  • Use worked solutions to learn the steps, then try similar problems yourself.
  • Cite what you use, the same way you would cite a book. The CiteMe tool can build a reference for you.

3What we do not support

  • Handing in someone else's work, or a lightly edited copy of it, as your own.
  • Sharing live exam questions, or material your instructor has told you not to share.
  • Using AI output as a finished assignment where your school forbids it.

4For instructors

If you teach and find your course material, exam content or a student's confidential work in the library, tell us through the contact page with the document address. We treat integrity reports as a priority and will remove material that should not be public. You can also ask us to block a specific title from being uploaded again.

5About the AI Detector

The detector estimates how likely a passage is to be machine written. It can be wrong in both directions, so please do not use a score alone to accuse a student. Talk to the student and look at the work in context.
