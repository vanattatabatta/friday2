Privacy Policy

What we collect when you use Expert Help, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1The short version

We collect only what we need to run your account, show you documents, take payments and keep the library safe. We do not sell your personal details, and we never see your full card number.

2What we collect

Details you give us, and details that arrive automatically when you use the site:

  • Account details: your name, email address, the User ID you choose and, if you add one, a phone number. If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Uploads: the files you add to the Doc Library, their titles and page counts, and a record of when and from where they were uploaded.
  • Usage details: which documents you unlock or download, your unlock balance, and messages you send through the chat box or contact form.
  • Technical details: your IP address, browser type and cookies that keep you signed in and help us spot abuse.

3How we use it

  • Run your account, sign you in and send the confirmation and service emails you need.
  • Show previews, apply unlocks, process purchases and answer your questions.
  • Check uploads for duplicates, spam and personal data, and act on copyright notices.
  • Protect the site from fraud and misuse, and improve how the tools work.

4Who else sees it

Some of your information passes through companies that help us run the service: a payment provider for checkout, an email provider for messages, Google when you use Google sign-in, and AI providers when you send text to a tool such as Ask AI or the AI Detector. They may only use it to provide their part of the service. We may also share information when the law requires it or to answer a valid copyright notice.

5Cookies

We use a small number of cookies. One keeps you signed in, and another lets a visitor who has not signed up yet keep the unlocks earned by uploading. Blocking cookies in your browser will stop sign-in from working.

6How long we keep it

We keep account details while your account exists. Upload records are kept longer on purpose, because they are our evidence when a rights holder contacts us. Payment records are kept as long as tax rules require.

7Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal details, or close your account, by writing to us through the contact page. Where a document has already been published and others rely on it, we may remove your name from it instead of deleting the document. If you live somewhere with extra privacy rights, we will honour them.

8Children

Expert Help is meant for students aged 13 and over. If you believe a younger child has given us personal details, tell us and we will remove them.

9Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for big changes, tell you by email or on the site.
