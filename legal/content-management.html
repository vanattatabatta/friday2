1Checks on every upload

As soon as a file arrives, the system reads its pages and compares them with what is already in the library. It looks for exact copies, close matches, link-selling and search-engine spam, and personal details such as ID numbers, bank or tax records and medical information.

2What visitors see

  • Every document shows a preview of the top part of its pages; the rest is blurred until it is unlocked.
  • Documents that look like they hold personal details are not shown to search engines and show no readable preview at all.
  • Scanned pages are read with text recognition in the background so they can be searched.

3Who can remove a document

Uploaders cannot delete a document once it is published, because other members may already have unlocked it. Removal is done by our team, either on a rights holder's notice, a report under the Community Guidelines or a policy check. If you need your own upload removed, ask us through the contact page.

4Records we keep

For every upload we keep the file, who sent it and when, the network address it came from and a fingerprint of the file. We use these records to answer notices, stop repeat copies and protect members from fraud.

5Asking us to act

Use the contact page to report a document, request removal of your own content, ask for a title to be blocked, or appeal a decision. Tell us the document address and what you would like us to do, and we will reply by email.
