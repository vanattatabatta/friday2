2Your account

  • Give accurate details and keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

3Unlocks, purchases and refunds

Every document shows a short preview. To read and download the rest you can pay for that document, use an unlock, or hold an active plan.

  • One unlock opens one document. Once opened, it stays unlocked on your account and can be downloaded again at no extra cost.
  • Unlocks are earned by uploading original documents of three or more pages, in the amounts shown on the upload page. Duplicates, refused files and very short files do not count.
  • Packs of unlocks expire after the period shown at checkout. Unused unlocks in an expired pack are lost.
  • Because digital documents are delivered the moment you unlock them, purchases are not refundable once opened, except where the law says otherwise or the file is plainly broken. Write to us and we will make it right.

4What you may upload

Upload only work you created or have permission to share. You keep ownership of what you upload, and you give us a worldwide, non-exclusive licence to host it, show a preview, offer it to other members and let them download it after unlocking.

  • Do not upload anything copied from textbooks, paid courses, exams under embargo or another person's private work.
  • Do not upload files that exist mainly to push links, sell services or game search results.
  • Do not upload documents holding other people's personal, medical or financial details.

5Using the site

  • Do not scrape, bulk download, resell or republish library content.
  • Do not try to read the hidden part of a preview or get around an unlock.
  • Do not use the tools to hand in work as your own where your school forbids it. See our Academic Integrity page.
  • Do not attack, overload or probe the service.
